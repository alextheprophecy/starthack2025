The story

From hackathon case to the finals stage

At START Hack 2025, Virgin challenged teams to get more people engaged with the causes run across the group: Virgin Unite, Virgin Atlantic, Virgin Voyages, Virgin Media O2 and more. We built a platform where every contribution becomes something you can see, share and be proud of. As The Alex Team, we won the Virgin case and earned a pitch in the finals on the START Summit main stage, in front of the audience and a panel of investors.

What it does

Impact you can see

Doing good shouldn't vanish into a confirmation email. We turn it into progress you can watch grow.

🌱 Your own forest

Every initiative you join plants something new in a perspective-rendered meadow. Items spring in one by one and are placed by a seeded procedural layout, so your forest is unique and the same on every visit.

🌳 TreeEnvironmental
🌻 SunflowerSocial impact
🔆 Solar panelInnovation
⭐ StarfishSpecial reward

✨ Virgin Points, rewarded

Your dashboard counts your points up live and splits them by category. You can spend them in the Virgin ecosystem.

  • Live count-up for total and per-category points
  • Redeem through Virgin Red or at Virgin Hotels
  • A full history of every contribution you've made

👫 Grow it together

Doing good is more fun with friends. Switch the friends page to Forest View to compare everyone's forest and see who is making waves.

  • Each friend's forest is generated from their own contributions
  • Initiative pages show which friends are already taking part
  • This view was on the big screen during our finals pitch

🐢 From discovery to action in one tap

You get personalised recommendations first, then every initiative grouped by Virgin company. Initiative pages explain the challenge and what Virgin is doing, and give you ways to act right away.

  • Find local volunteering on a map, such as beach clean-ups
  • One-tap donations that unlock special forest rewards
  • Internal mode lets Virgin employees publish new challenges

Under the hood

How it's built

Next.js 15 + React 19

App Router, TypeScript and API routes for users, friends and challenges, backed by JSON for hackathon speed.

Tailwind 4 + Framer Motion

Styling in Virgin red, animated card entrances, spring transitions and a live points count-up.

A forest without WebGL

CSS 3D transforms (perspective + rotateX), a seeded PRNG for layout, and depth-based scaling and z-ordering.
